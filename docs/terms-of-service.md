## 5. Features that estimate or check things

Some features interpret your mail on your device and can be wrong:

- **Phishing and safety warnings** help you spot suspicious mail. They
  can miss a dangerous message or flag a harmless one. Always use your
  own judgement before opening links or attachments.
- **Subscriptions**, their prices, totals and next charge dates are
  estimated from receipts and bills in your mail. Check the original
  receipt or your bank statement before relying on them.
- **Verification codes** shown in the list are read from the message;
  check the message itself if in doubt.
- **Unsubscribing** sends the request the sender asked for; whether they
  honour it is up to them.
