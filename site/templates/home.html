Inbox & focus

Privacy & security

Warnings that say why, and nothing that watches you

Every email is checked on your device, even when it comes from a real, verified address. The warning lists what looks wrong, so you can decide for yourself.

Blocked by default

Remote images and trackers wait until you load them. Read receipts are never sent, and tracking tags are taken off links.

A report of what was kept

The Privacy report counts the trackers, images and tracking tags kept from senders, and the read receipts not sent.

Locked down

Encrypted storage, an app lock with PIN or fingerprint, and end-to-end encrypted sync of your settings between devices.

Only what you turn on

Your mail goes between your device and your provider. Link checks and the AI assistant stay off until you add them.

Questions

Anything else? The project page has the details, and the privacy policy says exactly how your data is handled.

Is it really free?

Yes. No ads, no subscription and nothing to unlock. The code is open source under the Mozilla Public License 2.0.

Does my mail go through a Citusmail server?

No. There is no Citusmail server. The app connects to your own mail provider, and to other services only when you tap something or turn it on.

Which mail providers work?

Gmail, Outlook / Hotmail and Microsoft 365 with their own sign-in, and any provider with IMAP and SMTP. Connections are always encrypted.

Do I need the AI features?

No. They are off until you add your own key for a provider you choose, and the app works fully without them. With a key, only the email you ask about is sent.

Is there an iPhone or Mac version?

Not at the moment: Citusmail is made for Android, Windows and Linux.
