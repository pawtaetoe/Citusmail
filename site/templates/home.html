Citus is Latin for fast

Fast, private email that stays yours

A free, open-source mail client for Linux, Windows and Android. No subscriptions, no ads, no tracking, and no server of ours: your mail goes only between your device and your mail provider.

Works with Gmail, Outlook and any IMAP/SMTP provider.

What sets it apart

Your subscriptions, found in your mail

Paid subscriptions send a receipt every time they charge you, but they're easy to forget. Citusmail reads your receipts, bills and renewal emails on your device and lists every subscription it finds:

  • what you spend a year, a month and a day, and how it splits between your subscriptions;
  • each one's price, how often it charges and the next charge;
  • every past receipt, opening right in place, and when the price went up;
  • phone, broadband and utility bills too, with their average when they vary.

Codes in the list

One-time codes, sign-in links and password resets get their own section, and the code shows without opening the email. Copy it with a tap.

Phishing checks that read the message

Besides forged senders and lookalike links, Citusmail warns when an outsider writes in the name of your own school or company, or a big brand, especially with hidden recipients or a form "portal".

No server of ours

Everything runs on your device. There is no Citusmail account, cloud or tracking, and your mail is stored encrypted.

Everything you need from a mail app

  • Smart Inbox. Mail sorted on your device into Pinned, Verification, Important, General, Notifications and Newsletters.
  • Several accounts in one inbox. Any IMAP/SMTP provider, Gmail and Outlook / Microsoft 365, with their own sign-in.
  • Privacy by default. Images and trackers blocked, read receipts never sent, tracking removed from links, one-tap unsubscribe, and blocking senders.
  • Writing mail. Drafts, replies, undo send, custom folders and rules.
  • Every message, whole. Save it as an .eml file, see its original headers and source, or forward it as an attachment.
  • Encrypted everywhere. Encrypted storage, passwords in the system keychain, end-to-end encrypted sync of settings between your devices, and an app lock with PIN or fingerprint.

On phones, the menu, search, New message and Send sit at the bottom of the screen, within reach of one thumb.

Your privacy

Citusmail has no account with us and no server of ours. We don't collect, receive, store, sell or share your data, and there's no analytics, crash reporting or advertising. Your mail, contacts and settings stay on your device, encrypted, and travel only between your device and your own mail provider, plus optional services you turn on yourself.
