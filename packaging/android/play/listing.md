## Full description (4000 characters at most)

Citusmail is a free, open-source mail app that keeps your mail yours.
There is no Citusmail account, no cloud and no tracking: the app talks
only to your own mail provider, and everything else happens on your
phone.

SMART INBOX
Your mail is sorted on your phone into Pinned, Verification, Important,
General, Notifications and Newsletters. Sign-in codes show right in the
list and in the notification, copied with one tap.

FOUND IN YOUR MAIL
• Subscriptions: what you spend a year, a month and a day, each
  subscription's price, next charge and past receipts.
• Bills and free trials coming up, with a reminder before they charge.
• Deliveries with tracking, and return-by dates.
• Trips: flights, hotels and bookings put together, with local times.
• Online accounts: the services you have signed up to.

PRIVACY BY DEFAULT
• Images and trackers blocked; read receipts never sent.
• Tracking removed from links; one-tap unsubscribe.
• Phishing warnings that explain what is wrong: forged senders,
  lookalike links, outsiders writing in your company's name.
• A privacy report of everything kept from senders.

INBOX TOOLS
Snooze, Send later, "remind me if no reply", a Screener for first-time
senders, and clean-up of busy senders.

INSTANT MAIL
New mail arrives within seconds over a live connection to your own mail
server, shown by a quiet notification. You can turn it off in Settings.

EVERY ACCOUNT IN ONE INBOX
Gmail, Outlook / Microsoft 365 and any IMAP/SMTP provider.

LOCKED DOWN
Mail stored encrypted on your phone, passwords in the system keychain,
an app lock with PIN or fingerprint, and end-to-end encrypted sync of
settings between your devices.

Free, no ads, no subscriptions. Source code:
https://github.com/pawtaetoe/Citusmail
Privacy policy: https://citusmail.fluminaris.com/privacy-policy
